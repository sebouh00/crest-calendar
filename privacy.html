Privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

What Crest Calendar accesses

With your permission, Crest Calendar reads the events on your primary Google Calendar, using the read-only calendar.events.readonly scope. It reads the next day of events: each event's title, start and end time, response status, video call link, location, description and link to the event.

How the data is used

The events are used only to show your next meeting in the Crest browser's sidebar and to open its video call when you click Join. Crest Calendar never changes, creates or deletes events.

Where the data goes

Crest Calendar runs entirely on your Mac. Calendar data travels only between Google and the Crest app on your Mac, is kept in memory while Crest runs, and is not written to disk, sent to any other server, sold or shared with anyone. There is no Crest Calendar server.

The only thing stored is the sign-in Google grants (a refresh token), kept in your Mac's keychain so Crest can keep reading your calendar, plus the name of the connected calendar.

Removing access

Disconnect the account in Crest's settings, which deletes the stored sign-in and asks Google to revoke it, or remove Crest's access at any time from your Google Account permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Crest Calendar's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
